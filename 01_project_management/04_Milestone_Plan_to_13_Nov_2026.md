## 3. Minimum content to bring to the milestone presentation

The presentation and demo should make it clear:

- What business problem and question the project addresses.
- Why the Olist dataset is suitable and what source files are being used.
- What has been learned from initial data profiling and EDA.
- What the initial ERD / warehouse schema looks like and what the SQL DDL defines.
- Which assumptions, data limitations, open questions and risks remain.
- What feedback was received and what decisions or actions are required after the milestone.

## 4. Readiness review

Before the presentation, the team should check that:

- All milestone files are in the shared repository or an agreed shared location.
- Each output has an owner and can be explained by that owner.
- EDA figures and conclusions can be reproduced from the notebook.
- ERD and DDL are consistent with the available source data and current definitions.
- The demo has been tested on the equipment that will be used for the presentation.
- A feedback log is ready to capture comments, decisions and follow-up actions.

## 5. Boundary of this plan

The proposal lists additional work after Week 46. It is deliberately not expanded in this version. After the 13 November presentation, the team can update the timeline and add the next phase based on feedback and confirmed priorities.
