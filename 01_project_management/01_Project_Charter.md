## 2. Project background

Olist's data is distributed across related source files. The project proposal identifies a need for a single, trusted view of how sales, delivery performance and customer satisfaction relate. In particular, the effect of late deliveries on customer review scores has not yet been quantified in the proposed analysis.

## 3. Business problem

The project will bring the available order, delivery, sales and review information together so that the team can examine the relationships between delivery performance, customer satisfaction and sales.

The project will use the Olist e-commerce dataset and Business Intelligence methods to build an organised data foundation and produce analysis that can support business understanding.

## 4. Main project question

**How does delivery performance affect customer satisfaction and sales?**

The wording above follows the project proposal title. The analysis should report relationships supported by the data and avoid presenting an observed relationship as proof of causation.

## 5. Intended value

- Combine related source data into a structured analytical foundation.
- Define measurable business KPIs.
- Explore delivery performance and customer review patterns.
- Present useful findings through Business Intelligence outputs.
- Explore whether available order information can help identify orders at risk of a 1–2-star review.

## 6. Dataset baseline

The proposal identifies the public Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset from Kaggle. It describes the dataset as approximately **99,441 orders**, **9 source CSV files** and approximately **1.55 million rows**. These figures are proposal-level figures and should be checked against the downloaded files during acquisition and profiling.

The proposal's analysis scope covers orders from **January 2017 to August 2018**. It also describes the source as static, anonymised historical data from 2016–2018.

Dataset source listed in the proposal: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce
